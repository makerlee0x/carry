How accounting works now
1. Deposit senior credits that lender's seniorIdle. withdrawSenior pulls only from their idle (plus claimable yield/MSTR).
2. Matching walks idle lenders in deposit order, fills open juniors, and records funder slices (lender, amount, matchedAt) on the position.
3. At claimFees / earlyExit / settle, senior fee share and SellShares MSTR go only to that position's funders, weighted by amount x time matched on that position.
4. On close, each funder's matched principal returns to their own idle (or stays in vault under auto-compound).
5. IdleCarry burns only the junior's own idle. No cross-lender contamination.
