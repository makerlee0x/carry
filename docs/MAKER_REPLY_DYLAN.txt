How the pool works (brief)
1. depositSenior mints shares against totalSeniorAssets.
2. Matching moves idle into reservedSenior (NAV unchanged).
3. Yield compounds into totalSeniorAssets (share price up).
4. withdrawSenior is instant if idle covers and queue empty; else enqueues; claimWithdraw pays wallet when fulfilled.
