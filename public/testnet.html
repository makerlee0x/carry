Gas credit & auto-compound

writes to chain
Your gas credit—
Auto-compound—

Gas credit: a junior prefunds ETH. While your position is first in the waiting queue, each lender deposit pays that lender a gas refund from it. Unused credit comes back when you withdraw Idle stock, settle or early-exit.

Auto-compound: when on, exits and lender withdrawals reopen as new Idle positions instead of paying your wallet. Stock becomes a new junior position; USDG becomes idle lender capital.
